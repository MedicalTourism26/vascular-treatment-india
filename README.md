# vascular-treatment-india
Vascular diseases affect the blood vessels, including arteries, veins, and lymphatic vessels. These conditions can interfere with normal blood circulation and may require timely diagnosis, medical management, or specialised surgical treatment.

India has several specialised hospitals and experienced vascular surgeons offering diagnostic and treatment facilities for a wide range of vascular conditions. Treatment is selected according to the patient's diagnosis, medical history, severity of the condition, and overall health.
Common Vascular Conditions

Some commonly treated vascular conditions include:

Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT): Formation of blood clots in deep veins, commonly in the legs.

Varicose Veins: Enlarged and twisted veins that may cause pain, swelling, or discomfort.

Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD): Reduced blood flow due to narrowing of the arteries.

Aortic Aneurysm: Abnormal enlargement of a section of the aorta.

Carotid Artery Disease: Narrowing of the arteries supplying blood to the brain.

Chronic Venous Insufficiency: A condition in which veins have difficulty returning blood to the heart.

Vascular Treatment Options

Depending on the condition, treatment may include medication, lifestyle modifications, minimally invasive procedures, or surgery. Common procedures include angioplasty, stenting, endovenous laser treatment, thrombectomy, and vascular bypass surgery.

The appropriate treatment approach is determined after specialist consultation and necessary investigations.
